ParaView ex06 Large data

A 5.7 GB cyclone simulation drawn as slices, terrain, volume rendering and wind, in parallel.

Reads
A WRF simulation of Cyclone Chapala (2015) and a rainfall file, 5.7 GB together; skipped where the data is not installed.
Does
Slices coloured by water vapour; terrain raised by height; rain and ice volume-rendered; surface wind as line-integral convolution (a streaky texture that follows the flow) with arrows; rainfall on top. Rendered in parallel with OSPRay (the batch scripts use 8 ranks).
Writes
One 2850 × 1750 PNG.
Checked
The image against its baseline, allowing 5% of pixels to differ because the wind texture varies from run to run.
