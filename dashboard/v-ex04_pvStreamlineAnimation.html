ParaView ex04 Streamlines

343 streamlines traced through a vector field, growing over 125 frames.

Reads
The file data/noise.silo (a 50 × 50 × 50 grid; the field hardyglobal) and its vector field grad.
Does
Seeds a 7 × 7 × 7 grid of 343 points and traces streamlines forward through grad, drawn as tubes over three slices of hardyglobal; each frame lets the lines run one more step. Rendered with OSPRay ray tracing.
Writes
125 PNGs (4054 × 2536); an MP4 movie on a workstation.
Checked
The run must finish; the first five frames are compared with baseline images, allowing 0.5% of pixels to differ (GPU line drawing varies slightly).
