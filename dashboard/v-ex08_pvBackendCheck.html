ParaView ex08 Backend check

Fails when a job that asked for a GPU renders in software.

Reads
Nothing: it makes a sphere.
Does
Asks the OpenGL driver who it is (vendor, renderer, version) and calls it software (llvmpipe, swrast and similar) or hardware (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel); then renders a small probe image.
Writes
One probe PNG; the driver strings go into the record's notes.
Checked
A job that asked for a GPU (EGL) must get hardware, one that asked for OSMesa must get software, and the probe must be the requested size.
