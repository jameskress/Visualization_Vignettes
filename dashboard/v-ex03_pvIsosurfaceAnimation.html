ParaView ex03 Isosurface animation

An isosurface (the surface where a field equals one value) swept from 2.0 to 5.4 over 35 frames.

Reads
The file data/noise.silo (a 50 × 50 × 50 grid; the field hardyglobal), through ParaView's VisIt Silo reader (skipped on a build without it).
Does
Contours hardyglobal at 35 values, 2.0 to 5.4 in steps of 0.1, one frame each.
Writes
35 PNGs (4054 × 2536); an MP4 movie on a workstation.
Checked
The run must finish; the first five frames are compared with baseline images.
